Frequently Asked Questions

How long does GitHub org access take?

Usually within 24 hours. Automated requests are reviewed daily.

Do I need coding experience?

Not necessarily! We have opportunities for hardware, documentation, research, and clinical contributors.

Can I contribute in multiple areas?

Absolutely! Many contributors work across pathways.

Is there mentorship available?

Yes, we match new contributors with experienced mentors in Discord.
