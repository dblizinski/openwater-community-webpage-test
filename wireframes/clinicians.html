Section 2: Clinical Feedback & Input

Content needed: Clinical engagement guidelines from stakeholders (500 words)

Why Your Input Matters

Clinicians provide invaluable perspective that shapes device development:

  • Workflow Integration: Ensure devices fit into clinical practice
  • User Interface Design: Make controls intuitive and safe
  • Clinical Protocols: Develop evidence-based treatment protocols
  • Safety Monitoring: Identify potential risks and contraindications
  • Patient Experience: Optimize for patient comfort and outcomes

Feedback Collection Process

  1. Surveys & Questionnaires: Quick feedback on specific features
  2. User Testing Sessions: Hands-on evaluation of devices
  3. Focus Groups: Group discussions on clinical needs
  4. Advisory Board: Ongoing strategic input
  5. Discord #clinicians: Continuous informal feedback

Clinical Advisory Board

Join our advisory board to provide ongoing strategic guidance:

  • Quarterly meetings (virtual or in-person)
  • Review development roadmap
  • Advise on clinical priorities
  • Guide regulatory strategy
  • Recognition in community

How to Participate

As Principal Investigator:

  1. Review study protocols and requirements
  2. Submit site interest form
  3. Complete site feasibility assessment
  4. Obtain local IRB approval
  5. Receive training and equipment
  6. Enroll and treat patients

As Site Collaborator:

  • Support existing PI at your institution
  • Assist with patient recruitment
  • Participate in data collection

Proposing a New Study

Have an idea for a clinical study? We can help:

  1. Submit study concept form
  2. Schedule consultation with research team
  3. Develop protocol collaboratively
  4. Access equipment and technical support
  5. IRB/ethics support and templates
