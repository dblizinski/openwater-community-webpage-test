Your Quick Start Guide

👨‍💻 For Developers

  1. Fork and Clone: Fork our repository and set up your local development environment
  2. Set Up Dev Environment: Follow our contributor guide to install dependencies and build from source
  3. Find a Good First Issue: Browse our GitHub issues labeled "good first issue" or "beginner-friendly"
  4. Make Your Contribution: Create a feature branch, make your changes, and submit a pull request
  5. Get Code Review: Engage with maintainers in the review process and refine your contribution

🔬 For Researchers

  1. Review Collaboration Policy: Understand our research collaboration framework and IP guidelines
  2. Schedule Consultation: Book a call with our research team to discuss your interests
  3. Access Documentation: Get technical specs, datasets, and research protocols
  4. Explore Model Commons: Browse shared datasets and models from our research community
  5. Submit Proposal: Propose a joint study, validation study, or method development project

🏥 For Clinicians

  1. Complete Interest Survey: Tell us about your clinical practice and interests
  2. Schedule Consultation: Meet with our clinical team to discuss opportunities
  3. Review Regulatory Info: Understand clinical status, regulatory pathway, and safety data
  4. Join Advisory Board: Participate in strategic guidance and development planning
  5. Participate in Studies: Get involved in clinical trials and feedback opportunities

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does GitHub org access take? ▼
Usually within 24 hours. Automated requests are reviewed daily. If you haven't received access within 24 hours, please reach out to our community team at community@openwater.health.
Do I need coding experience to contribute? ▼
Not necessarily! We have opportunities for developers, hardware engineers, technical writers, researchers, and clinicians. We welcome all skill levels and provide mentorship for those new to open source.
Can I contribute in multiple pathways? ▼
Absolutely! Many of our contributors work across multiple pathways. For example, some developers also participate in clinical feedback, or researchers contribute documentation. You're welcome to engage in any areas that interest you.
Is mentorship available for new contributors? ▼
Yes! We match new contributors with experienced mentors in our Discord community. Our #mentors channel helps pair you with someone who can guide you through your first contributions.
What's the time commitment required? ▼
Flexible! Contributions can range from one-off code reviews or feedback to ongoing research collaboration. You decide how much time you can invest. Many contributors start with small tasks and increase involvement over time.
How do I get recognized for my contributions? ▼
We recognize contributors through GitHub contributor graphs, our community contributors page, blog features, and citations in academic publications. Active contributors may also be invited to speak at webinars or present at conferences.
