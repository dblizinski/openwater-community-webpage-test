What is Openwater?

🔬 Technology Overview

Technology overview in non-technical language (500 words)

Openwater develops open-source platforms for medical imaging and neuromodulation with focus on democratizing advanced medical technology through collaborative research and development. Our core mission is to make cutting-edge medical technologies accessible to researchers worldwide while maintaining scientific rigor and patient safety.

Core Technologies

  • OpenLIFU: Focused ultrasound for brain imaging and therapy with precision targeting
  • OpenMOTION: Motion tracking for medical applications in rehabilitation and diagnostics

🧠 Research Applications

  • Neuroimaging and brain stimulation
  • Cancer treatment (oncology)
  • Vascular imaging (blood flow)
  • Motion analysis for rehabilitation

🎯 Current Research Focus

List of active research areas from stakeholders
  • Low-intensity focused ultrasound for depression treatment
  • Real-time blood flow monitoring
  • Portable medical imaging devices
  • AI-enhanced image processing

Research Collaboration

Research collaboration policy and framework (500 words)

🤝 Types of Collaboration

  • Joint Studies: Collaborative research projects with shared goals
  • Platform Access: Use Openwater hardware/software for your research
  • Validation Studies: Help validate our technology in clinical settings
  • Method Development: Develop new imaging or stimulation protocols

📋 Partnership Process

  1. Submit research proposal or inquiry
  2. Schedule consultation with research team
  3. Review collaboration agreement
  4. Access technical resources and support
  5. Conduct research with ongoing collaboration
  6. Publish results with appropriate attribution

🔒 Data Sharing Policies

How we handle research data from stakeholders
  • Your data remains your property
  • Aggregated/anonymized data may be shared publicly
  • HIPAA compliance for clinical data
  • IRB approval coordination

✍️ Co-Authorship Guidelines

Clear expectations for publication from stakeholders
  • Standard academic authorship principles apply
  • Openwater team members may be co-authors based on contribution
  • Platform acknowledgment required in publications
  • Pre-publication review process (optional)

⚖️ IP Considerations

Intellectual property framework from stakeholders
  • Open-source licensing (AGPL-3.0 transitioning to Apache 2.0)
  • Your research findings and methods remain yours
  • Platform improvements may be contributed back to project
  • Patent considerations for novel inventions

Model Commons

🏛️ What is Model Commons?

A centralized repository for research data, models, and protocols in the Openwater ecosystem. Share your datasets, trained models, and experimental protocols with the research community while maintaining control and attribution.

📤 Publishing Your Work

  1. Prepare your research data or models
  2. Add metadata and comprehensive documentation
  3. Submit via GitHub or web portal
  4. Community review and publication

📥 Accessing Research

Browse available datasets, trained models, and protocols from fellow researchers:

  • Imaging datasets for benchmarking
  • Stimulation protocols and parameters
  • Analysis pipelines and code
  • Calibration data and reference materials

📖 Citation Standards

Properly credit Openwater and shared datasets in your publications. We provide citation templates and guidelines to make attribution simple and consistent.
