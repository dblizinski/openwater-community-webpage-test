Clinical Applications

🏥 Technology Overview for Clinicians

Openwater develops open-source platforms for medical imaging and neuromodulation designed with clinical practice in mind. Our technology addresses real clinical needs while prioritizing patient safety, ease of use, and regulatory compliance.

Core Clinical Platforms

  • OpenLIFU: Focused ultrasound for non-invasive brain imaging and therapy
    • Depression and anxiety treatment
    • Essential tremor management
    • Tumor ablation and thermal therapy
    • Blood-brain barrier opening for drug delivery
  • OpenMOTION: Precision motion tracking for clinical applications
    • Rehabilitation monitoring and assessment
    • Surgical guidance and accuracy
    • Movement disorder assessment and tracking

✅ Clinical Benefits

  • Non-invasive or minimally invasive procedures
  • Real-time feedback and monitoring
  • Personalized treatment protocols
  • Cost-effective compared to traditional options
  • Open-source = transparent and reproducible
  • Evidence-based and clinically validated

🛡️ Patient Safety

Safety is our top priority. All Openwater platforms undergo rigorous safety testing and validation:

  • Comprehensive safety testing protocols
  • Known contraindications documentation
  • Adverse event reporting and tracking
  • Quality management system compliance
  • Regular safety audits and updates
  • Patient informed consent support

Clinical Feedback & Input

Why Your Input Matters

Content needed from stakeholders: Clinical engagement guidelines and best practices (500 words)

Clinicians provide invaluable perspective that fundamentally shapes device development. Your expertise directly influences how our technology integrates into real clinical workflows:

  • Workflow Integration: Ensure devices seamlessly fit into your clinical practice and improve efficiency
  • User Interface Design: Make controls intuitive, safe, and aligned with clinical conventions
  • Clinical Protocols: Develop evidence-based treatment protocols and best practices
  • Safety Monitoring: Identify potential risks, contraindications, and edge cases
  • Patient Experience: Optimize for patient comfort, outcomes, and satisfaction
  • Training Requirements: Define necessary training and competency assessment for users

📋 Feedback Collection Process

  1. Surveys & Questionnaires: Quick feedback on specific features and usability
  2. User Testing Sessions: Hands-on evaluation and direct interaction with devices
  3. Focus Groups: Group discussions on clinical needs and implementation strategies
  4. Advisory Board: Ongoing strategic input and guidance
  5. Discord #clinicians: Real-time community feedback and discussion

Clinical Studies & Trials

📊 Active Clinical Studies

Content needed from stakeholders: Current active clinical studies and recruitment status

Currently recruiting clinicians and sites for:

  • Low-intensity focused ultrasound for treatment-resistant depression (Phase II)
  • Motion tracking for Parkinson's disease assessment (Pilot Study)
  • Portable ultrasound imaging validation and optimization (Feasibility)

🎯 How to Participate

As Principal Investigator:

  1. Review study protocols and site requirements
  2. Submit site interest and feasibility form
  3. Complete institutional assessment
  4. Obtain local IRB/ethics approval
  5. Receive training and equipment setup
  6. Enroll and manage patients in study

As Site Collaborator:

  • Support existing PI at your institution
  • Assist with patient recruitment
  • Participate in data collection

💡 Proposing a New Study

Have a clinical study idea? We provide support for study design and implementation:

  1. Submit study concept form with clinical background
  2. Schedule consultation with clinical research team
  3. Develop protocol collaboratively with our team
  4. Access equipment and technical support
  5. Receive IRB/ethics support and templates
