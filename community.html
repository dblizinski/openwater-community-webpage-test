About Openwater

Our Mission

Openwater is democratizing medical device development by bringing together a global community of engineers, researchers, and clinicians to advance open-source medical technology.

Our Focus

We specialize in developing innovative medical devices through open collaboration. Our platforms enable rapid prototyping, research validation, and clinical implementation of breakthrough technologies in ultrasound, neuromodulation, and advanced diagnostics.
